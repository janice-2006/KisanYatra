🌾 Kisan Yatra

Kisan Yatra is an intelligent, AI-driven agricultural platform designed to bridge the gap between technology and grassroots farming. Developed as a hands-on project to test and expand knowledge in machine learning, dataset training, and computer vision, this platform empowers farmers with accurate disease diagnosis, multilingual audio guidance, and market connectivity.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **AI-Powered Crop Disease Detection:** Accurately diagnoses crop health and plant diseases from uploaded leaf images, returning confidence scores and precise treatment methods.
* **Multilingual & Audio Support:** Fully localizes descriptions, confidence ratings, and treatment steps into multiple regional Indian languages—complete with voice-over audio translation when a language option is selected.
* **Trader Routing & Market Connectivity:** Connects farmers directly to nearby traders and marketplaces for their harvested crops.
* **Soil & Crop Analysis:** Intelligent recommendations tailored to environmental and agricultural parameters.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Frontend:** Streamlit (Python) for a fast, responsive, and interactive user interface.
* **Backend & API:** FastAPI for handling application logic, routing, and backend services.
* **Machine Learning & CV:** Custom-trained datasets and computer vision models for image classification and real-time plant disease identification.
* **Internationalization (i18n):** Dynamic dictionary mapping for seamless multi-language translation and accessibility.

---

## 🔮 Future Updates & Roadmap

This project serves as an ongoing exploration of applied machine learning and agricultural technology. Upcoming updates and features include:
* Expanding dataset training with more regional crop disease categories.
* Enhancing real-time object detection models for field-captured videos.
* Integrating advanced supply chain and pricing prediction tools.
